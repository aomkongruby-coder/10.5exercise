How to read PSI

PSI is the overall air pollution index; PM2.5 and PM10 are 24-hour readings, while gaseous pollutants are based on their respective reporting windows.

0–50 Good · Air quality is ideal
51–100 Moderate · Most people do not need special adjustments
101–200 Unhealthy · Sensitive groups should reduce outdoor activity
201–300 Very Unhealthy · Everyone should reduce outdoor activity
PM values are in μg/m³; CO is in mg/m³.
